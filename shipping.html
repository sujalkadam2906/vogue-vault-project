1. Shipping and Delivery

We provide standard domestic delivery across India. All orders are packed and dispatched from our fulfillment center within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation.

  • Metro Areas: 2 to 4 business days.
  • Non-Metro Areas: 4 to 7 business days.
  • Standard Shipping Fee: Complimentary on all orders during this release.

Once dispatched, tracking links are simulated within your customer session.

2. 15-Day Return Window

We accept returns of unwashed, unworn garments with all tags intact within 15 days of delivery. For hygiene reasons, innerwear and socks are final sale unless damaged upon arrival.

3. How to Initiate a Return

To request a return or size exchange, email customer support at [CONTACT EMAIL] with your order reference number and the reason for exchange. A courier pick-up will be scheduled within 2 business days.

4. Refunds

Following item inspection at our warehouse, approved refunds are initiated to the original payment source within 5 to 7 business days. Since this site is currently a demonstration store, mock transactions will not charge your bank account or card.
